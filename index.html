About me

A calm, curious mind

I'm Aryan, an 18-year-old autistic young man. I'm calm and quiet, and unique in my own way. I love coding and music, and I like to think beyond the box and explore new ideas.

My curiosity takes me from writing software to the biggest questions in science: how the quantum world behaves, what the universe is made of, and how the brain works. My dream is to become a scientist and a coder.

How I work best

  • A calm, quiet environment where I can focus
  • 1:1 support from someone I know and trust
  • Time to settle in new places. I do very well in familiar settings, and I need support to carry those skills into new environments.
  • Familiar routines and people to bridge the gap between settings
  • Music and headphones to help me stay calm and comfortable
✦

Trust is the key. I can sense whether a person feels confident or doubtful about my ability. When the people around me believe in me, I relax and show my true potential.
